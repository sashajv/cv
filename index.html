I came to medicine through nursing, and to urology from a school with no home urology program.

2013 – 2018

Lakehead University, Ontario

B.S. in Science and Nursing. First-generation university student.

Anguilla

Began medical school and led the British Red Cross student chapter, running five community health clinics for 300+ residents.

2020 – 2023

Frontline nursing, Thunder Bay

Returned to nursing during COVID-19: outbreak units in a 540-resident long-term care home, and supervising ~200 nurses at national testing sites.
